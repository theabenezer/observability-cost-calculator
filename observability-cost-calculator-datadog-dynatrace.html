How the estimate works

Every number comes from a public list price or a cited study. The model multiplies your host count by per-host list prices for the capabilities you picked, at one typical volume assumption per line, and compares that with IBM Concert priced at IBM Instana list. Discounts, contract terms and your real data volumes will move every figure.

Cloud and infrastructure savings are kept apart from the tool comparison. The three-year view is an illustration you control, not a forecast.
